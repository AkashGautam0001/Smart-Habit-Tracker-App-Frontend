## What HabitFlow does
1. Habit tracking with streaks, calendar heatmap, and gamification (XP, levels, achievements)
2. Pomodoro focus timer (25/5/15 min) that auto-logs sessions to learning subjects
3. Study analytics — time per subject, focus score, best hours, trend charts
4. AI coaching (Pro) — daily review and weekly study plan via Claude API
5. PWA — installable on mobile and desktop, works offline
